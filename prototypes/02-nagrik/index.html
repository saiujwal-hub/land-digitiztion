How it works

Five stages between a dusty register and a certified record.

Nothing enters the record-of-rights without passing all five — and every stage leaves an entry in the audit trail.

1Upload

Tehsil bundles of scans, legacy PDFs or photographs are intake-checked for quality.

2Extract

OCR and NLP read handwritten and printed text, field by field, with a confidence score each.

3Classify

Each document is typed — jamabandi, mutation, deed — and mapped to the record schema.

4Validate

Rules flag impossible dates, mismatched areas and low-confidence fields for human review.

5Certify

An officer approves; the record is signed and pushed to LRMS/GIS with a QR anyone can verify.

Trust

Built for the accountability a land record demands.

Land decisions outlive governments and software. Nagrik is designed so every value can be traced to its source, its reader, and its reviewer.

✓

A human for every doubt

Fields below the confidence threshold never auto-approve. A clerk confirms each one against the scan, and the officer signs off — with names on every decision.

✦

Sealed against tampering

Certified records carry an RSA-PSS 2048-bit signature over their canonical data. Any later edit breaks the seal — and the verification page says so.

▣

Verifiable at the counter

The QR on a certified record resolves over the local network, so a tehsil counter can confirm authenticity even where internet is unreliable.
