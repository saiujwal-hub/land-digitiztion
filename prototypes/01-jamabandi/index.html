From Bundles to Sealed Records

PROCEDURE / 2026 / REV-3
1

Upload & Scan

Tehsil bundles of scanned registers or legacy PDFs are uploaded with bundle-level metadata.

2

OCR / NLP Extraction

Handwritten Devanagari and printed text are read; fields like survey, khasra and khata numbers are parsed.

3

Classification

Documents are classified — jamabandi, khasra, mutation, sale deed — and mapped to the record schema.

4

Validation & Review

Automated checks flag low-confidence fields for clerk correction, then officer approval.

5

Sealed Record

Certified records are signed (RSA-PSS) and pushed to LRMS / GIS with a permanent audit trail.

Trust & Auditability

SEC-POL / 07

Cryptographic Sealing

Every certified record is signed with RSA-PSS 2048 / SHA-256. Any later alteration invalidates the seal and is reported at verification.

Human-in-the-Loop

No field below the confidence threshold enters the record unreviewed. Clerk corrections and officer decisions are attributed and timestamped.

Offline QR Verification

Certified records carry a QR code verifiable over local network — no internet required at the tehsil counter.
