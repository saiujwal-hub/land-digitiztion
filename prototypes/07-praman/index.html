Subject

A land record is a promise. We make it verifiable.

Praman digitizes legacy registers with OCR, then puts every uncertain field in front of a human being. What survives both is sealed with a cryptographic signature — and can be proven authentic at a counter, offline, years from now.

1.The chain of custody — five hands touch every record, each one signs.

A record reaches the database only after all five stages agree. The seal at the end is not a stamp — it is arithmetic that any verifier can repeat.

  1. 1.1Scanned intake

    Tehsil bundles arrive with source metadata: village, register type, year range, scanning crew.

    Records room staff

  2. 1.2Machine reading

    OCR/NLP extracts every field with a confidence score. Below threshold, it declines to guess.

    Extraction engine · v3.2

  3. 1.3Clerk verification

    A named clerk compares each flagged field against the scan and corrects or confirms it.

    Verification desk · per record

  4. 1.4Officer decision

    An officer reviews the full record and the correction history, then approves or rejects with reasons.

    Tehsildar / Circle officer

  5. 1.5Sealing & publication

    RSA-PSS 2048-bit signature over canonical data; QR issued; record pushed to LRMS/GIS.

    Attestation service · automatic

2.What the seal actually guarantees.

Four properties, each testable, none of them marketing.

  1. 2.1Integrity

    The signature covers the canonical record. Change one character of one field and verification fails — loudly, at the counter.

  2. 2.2Attribution

    Every correction and decision carries the name, role and timestamp of the person who made it. There is no anonymous edit.

  3. 2.3Non-repudiation

    Approvals are signed with the officer's key. The record cannot later claim it was never certified — or that someone else did.

  4. 2.4Offline verifiability

    Verification needs only the record, the public key and a device. The tehsil counter does not need the internet to trust the paper.
