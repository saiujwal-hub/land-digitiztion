Parcel-match accuracy

From village sheet to GIS parcel.

A scanned cadastral sheet is only useful when its parcels can be found, matched and trusted. Naksha carries each sheet through five geospatial stages before it joins the LRMS/GIS layer.

STAGE 01

Scan & Georeference

Village sheets are scanned at 400 dpi and pinned to control points from the survey-of-India grid.

STAGE 02

Register Reading

OCR reads the corresponding khasra register — owner, area, tenure — with per-field confidence.

STAGE 03

Parcel Matching

Survey and khasra numbers are matched between map sheet and register; mismatches are flagged.

STAGE 04

Human Validation

Clerks resolve mismatches against the scan; officers approve the corrected parcel record.

STAGE 05

GIS Publication

Certified parcels are sealed and published to the LRMS/GIS layer with a verifiable QR.

Aligned to the DILRMP GIS stack.

Naksha speaks the formats and identifiers the modernization programme already uses, so digitized records land in existing systems without translation loss.

FORMAT

Parcel Identifiers

Unique parcel IDs carry village · survey · sub-survey codes exactly as the RoR uses them.

FORMAT

Vector Publication

Certified boundaries export as OGC-standard vectors with attribution and seal metadata.

CONTROL

Coordinate Reference

Sheets are reprojected to the state's notified CRS; transformations are logged per sheet.

CONTROL

Change Audit

Every re-mosaic, correction and republication is versioned and attributable to an officer.
